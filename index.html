About Phread RV Repair

With 8 years of experience in RV repair and maintenance, Phread RV Repair is your trusted partner for all your recreational vehicle needs in Oklahoma City. Owner Fred and his team provide professional, reliable service for RVs, travel trailers, and 5th wheel campers.

We understand that your RV represents freedom and adventure. That's why we're committed to keeping you on the road with quality repairs and honest service. Available 24/7 for emergencies, we're here when you need us most.
